The Future of SOC Operations

Kalki is an Autonomous SOC Analyst Platform designed for the Smart India Hackathon. It addresses the critical issue of alert fatigue in modern cybersecurity environments by replacing manual log analysis with advanced Agentic AI.

🤖 Agentic AI Triage

Instead of hardcoded Regex rules, Kalki uses Cognitive AI (Groq Llama-3) to read raw network logs, determine the severity, and explain its reasoning in plain English.

🔗 Smart Correlation

Individual alerts are grouped based on Temporal and IP heuristics into full attack storylines (Incidents) mapped directly to the MITRE ATT&CK framework.

⚡ Real-Time Detection

Equipped with a live Windows Endpoint Agent, Kalki intercepts malicious DNS queries and Drive-By downloads instantly, pushing alerts via WebSockets.
